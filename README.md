
# Custom Hand-Shaped Ergonomic Mouse

An ultra-personalized ergonomic computer mouse engineered from a physical hand mold to eliminate wrist strain and fit my grip like a glove.

## Project Overview

Standard commercial mice force my hand into unnatural positions, leading to wrist fatigue and discomfort over extended working hours. To solve this, I designed a mouse around my hand rather than forcing my hand to adapt to a pre-made mouse.

By molding clay around my natural resting palm position, 3D scanning the shape, and modifying it in Fusion, I created a 3D-printed housing fitted with mouse hardware that provides 100% custom ergonomic support.

---

## How It Works & Process

1. **Ergonomic Clay Grip:** Squeezed a block of physical clay to capture the exact, pressure-free resting shape of my palm and fingers.
2. **3D Mesh Scanning:** Scanned the clay mold to convert the organic physical form into a digital 3D mesh model.
3. **CAD Engineering:** Cleaned up the mesh and hollowed out the interior in CAD, adding structural mounts for my PCB, optical sensor, switches, and scroll wheel.
4. **3D Printing & Post-Processing:** Sliced and 3D printed the shell components, followed by support removal and surface smoothing/sanding for maximum touch comfort.
5. **Hardware Integration:** Transferred the electronics into my custom shell and secured all components.
